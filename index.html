The New SAP Exam Format: Scenario-Based and System-Based Assessments

SAP replaced most of its multiple-choice certification exams with practical, open-book, performance-based assessments. Here is what the new format actually looks like, exam by exam, plus how to prepare for it.

What is the new SAP exam format?

The new SAP exam format replaces proctored multiple-choice exams with practical, open-book, performance-based assessments. Since November 2025, SAP has rolled out two assessment types: scenario-based exams where you role-play with an AI avatar, and system-based exams where you complete real tasks inside a working SAP system.

SAP changed its certification program because a multiple-choice exam can only prove that someone memorized facts. The new formats prove that a certified professional can actually do the work. You configure systems, troubleshoot issues, design solutions, or advise stakeholders, just as you would on a real project.

The rollout started in November 2025 with six pilot certifications, including the SAP BTP Administrator, SAP BTP Solution Architect, SAP Build Developer, SAP Generative AI Developer, SAP Business Data Cloud, and RISE with SAP Methodology. The remaining portfolio is transitioning through 2026. Every certification uses exactly one format, chosen by the nature of the role.

The two new assessment types

  • Scenario-Based Assessment (SBA): you interact with an AI avatar that plays a business stakeholder. You pitch solutions, defend your reasoning, and guide the conversation toward the right outcome. There is no fixed answer key, because your reasoning is what is scored.
  • System-Based Assessment (SyBA): you complete actual tasks inside a time-limited SAP system simulation. You configure components, run processes, build artifacts, or resolve issues. The exam measures execution fluency and best-practice application.

Key facts about the new exams

Open book

You may use the SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning, SAP Community, and AI assistants during the exam, the same way you would on a live project.

No live proctoring

Identity is verified through your SAP Universal ID instead of a live proctor. There is no test-room surveillance.

Time-boxed, practical

Exams run as one connected activity or a set of system tasks, not 80 questions in 180 minutes.

Cut scores vary

Each exam publishes its own passing threshold, commonly in the 50 to 70 percent range. Verify the current one before you book.

Valid for 12 months

Certifications are maintained through SAP stay-certified assessments, so the credential keeps pace with product updates.

Enhanced badge

The digital badge now highlights applied validation, not just that a test was passed.

Why SAP made this change

Three problems pushed SAP to redesign certification. Exam dumps and brain dumps let candidates pass without competence. Employers could not trust that a badge meant a person could deliver. And as SAP moved to cloud, AI, and continuous updates, a memorized answer key went stale within months. The new formats test judgment, execution, and communication, which is what a hiring manager actually needs.

For existing certificate holders, nothing changes immediately. You keep your certification and continue the standard stay-certified renewal process. The new practical format applies to first-time exam takers.

How the two new formats work

Scenario-based exams test how you think and communicate. System-based exams test how you execute. Technical roles get system-based exams; consulting, architecture, and advisory roles get scenario-based exams.

Scenario-Based Assessment (SBA)

You are placed in a realistic business situation and interact with an AI avatar that plays a stakeholder, such as a CFO, a Head of Data, a project sponsor, or a department manager. The avatar asks questions, raises objections, and pushes on your reasoning. You respond in conversation, defend your choices, and steer the discussion toward the right outcome.

There is no fixed answer key. You are scored on how well you reason through the scenario, across the published objectives for that exam. For some certifications, instead of a live conversation you can record a video response for expert review.

What a scenario exam looks like

You receive a use case, such as designing a target-state data architecture or pitching a transformation plan. The AI avatar plays the stakeholder and asks you to justify your approach. You must hold two conversations at once: the business one about value, and the technical one about design, configuration, or governance.

Typical characteristics

  • Format: single connected activity or a small set of scenario interactions
  • Delivery: remote, through the SAP Learning platform
  • Scoring: objective-based, with your own words quoted back as evidence
  • Languages: roleplay with AI guidance in many languages; video upload in English
  • Cut score: published per exam, commonly 50 to 60 percent

Roles that use SBA: solution architects, data architects, business transformation consultants, project managers, consultants who advise on processes, and presales specialists who position SAP solutions.

System-Based Assessment (SyBA)

You work inside a time-limited SAP system simulation and complete real tasks, exactly as you would on a project. You configure components, set up master data, build processes, run transactions, or resolve issues. The exam measures whether you can execute accurately and apply best practices in a live system.

Because the format is task-based, there is no answer key in the old sense. The system evaluates what you actually did: the configuration you produced, the process you completed, the error you fixed.

What a system exam looks like

You might be asked to set up a business role in SAP Central Business Configuration, configure a maintenance plan in Asset Management, create subaccounts and assign entitlements in the SAP BTP cockpit, or build and deploy an OData service as a backend developer. The task set mirrors a real work assignment.

Typical characteristics

  • Format: one or more hands-on activities inside the system simulation
  • Delivery: remote, through the SAP Learning platform
  • Scoring: based on task execution and correct application of best practices
  • Time box: set per exam, commonly around two hours of working time
  • Cut score: published per exam, commonly 60 to 70 percent

Roles that use SyBA: system administrators, backend developers, implementation consultants who configure modules, and administrators who run a solution in production.

How to find which format your exam uses

Go to the official SAP certification page for your exam and read the current exam details before you book. SAP publishes the format, the number of activities, the duration, the cut score, and the language options for every active version. If the page says Scenario Based Assessment, expect a roleplay. If it says System Based Assessment, expect hands-on tasks. A few exams, such as the SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition implementation exam, combine scenario-driven project situations with system-based configuration tasks, so the published format description matters.

This credential proves you can design a target-state data architecture on SAP Business Data Cloud, choosing between federation, replication, and zero-copy data sharing, and aligning the landscape to business goals.

Who it is for

  • Data architects and lead consultants
  • Platform designers and data governance leads
  • Professionals joining a Business Data Cloud delivery team

Why it matters now

SAP Business Data Cloud is SAP's unified data platform and the data layer of the autonomous enterprise. With the ECC end of mainstream maintenance in 2027, organizations are consolidating analytics and sharing data across SAP and non-SAP tools.

How to prepare for the new SAP exams

Preparation for the new formats is hands-on. Follow the official learning journey, practice in the system, and rehearse scenario reasoning until your responses are automatic under time pressure.
  1. Confirm your exam's current format. Go to the official SAP certification page for your exam and read the current version details: format, cut score, duration, languages, and prerequisites. Do this before you spend money on preparation.
  2. Complete the official learning journey. SAP publishes a learning journey for every certification with courses, units, and practical exercises. Work through it end to end, in order, before you look at anything else.
  3. Get hands-on system access. For system-based exams, practice the actual tasks in a working SAP system until the sequences feel automatic. For scenario-based exams, practice configuration and process flows enough to explain them confidently.
  4. Practice scenario reasoning. Explain your reasoning out loud. Record yourself answering "why" questions. Practice defending a design against objections, the way the AI avatar will.
  5. Rehearse under time pressure. Repeat full scenario or task sequences with a timer. The exam measures execution fluency, so hesitation on routine steps costs you points.
  6. Review your weak areas systematically. Keep a log of mistakes and correct reasoning. Revisit the underlying concept, then retry the task or scenario.
  7. Prepare your delivery setup. Verify your SAP Universal ID, browser, camera, and workspace before exam day. The exam runs remotely through the SAP Learning platform.
  8. Schedule early and track your renewal. Leave room for a retake. Track your certification validity, because SAP certifications are maintained through stay-certified assessments.

Frequently asked questions

Short, direct answers to the questions people search for most about the new SAP exam format.
What is the new SAP exam format?
The new SAP exam format replaces proctored multiple-choice exams with practical, open-book, performance-based assessments. Since November 2025, SAP has rolled out two types: scenario-based exams where you role-play with an AI avatar, and system-based exams where you complete real tasks inside a working SAP system. Each certification uses one format, chosen by the nature of the role.
Are SAP certification exams open book now?
Yes. The new performance-based exams are open book. You can use the SAP Help Portal, SAP Learning, SAP Community, and AI assistants during the exam, just as you would on a real project. Old-style proctored exams that are still active remain closed book, so always confirm your exam's current format.
How many questions are in the new SAP exams?
There is no fixed question count. Scenario-based exams use one connected activity or a small set of interactions. System-based exams use a set of hands-on tasks, for example 9 to 14 activities depending on the exam. Old-style exams typically used 80 multiple-choice questions in 180 minutes.
Is there proctoring in the new SAP exams?
No. The new practical exams use no live proctoring. Your identity is verified through your SAP Universal ID instead. Old-style proctored exams still exist for some certifications, so confirm the current delivery method on your exam's official page.
How long does a scenario-based SAP exam take?
Scenario-based exams are time-boxed, commonly around two hours of working time for the roleplay or recorded response, plus preparation time for the scenario. Some certifications allow up to a 24-hour window for the full assessment window. Check your exam page for the published duration.
Can I use AI tools during my SAP exam?
Yes. The new performance-based exams allow AI assistants, including SAP Joule, during the assessment, because you would use them on a real project. You still need the judgment to decide when and how to use them, which is part of what the exam measures.
What is a system-based SAP assessment?
A system-based assessment (SyBA) puts you inside a time-limited SAP system simulation. You complete real tasks, such as configuring components, setting up master data, running processes, or fixing issues. Technical and administrative roles, such as SAP BTP Administrator, backend developers, and implementation consultants, use this format.
What is a scenario-based SAP assessment with AI avatar roleplay?
A scenario-based assessment (SBA) places you in a role-play with an AI avatar acting as a business stakeholder. You pitch solutions, defend your reasoning, and steer the conversation to the right outcome. There is no fixed answer key; you are scored on how you reason through the scenario across the published objectives. Some certifications offer a video-upload alternative for expert review.
Do I need to retake my SAP certification if I am already certified?
No, not immediately. Existing certificate holders keep their certification and continue the standard stay-certified renewal process. The new practical format applies to first-time exam takers.
How hard are the new SAP exams compared to the old ones?
The new exams are demanding in a different way. The old format was hard because of volume and memorization. The new format is hard because it requires applied fluency: you must execute tasks and defend reasoning in real time. Candidates with genuine hands-on experience generally find them manageable, while candidates who only studied question banks tend to struggle.
How much does a new SAP certification exam cost?
SAP certification exam fees vary by region and product. Many certifications are priced around 500 to 600 USD per attempt, and SAP Learning Hub subscriptions can include multiple attempts per year. Check the current fee on the official SAP certification page for your exam.
How do I prepare for a scenario-based SAP exam?
Prepare by following the official learning journey, studying real-world cases, and practicing your reasoning out loud. Record yourself answering "why" questions and defending designs against objections. Then rehearse full scenarios under a timer, because the exam rewards fluency and calm execution, not perfect recall.
Which SAP certifications use system-based exams?
Technical and administrative roles use system-based exams, including the SAP BTP Administrator (C_ADBTP), the Backend Developer for SAP Cloud Application Programming Model (C_CPE), the Asset Management implementation consultant (C_S43), and the SAP S/4HANA Cloud Public Edition implementation consultant (C_S4CPB). Confirm each exam's current format on its official page.
Will exam dumps help me pass the new SAP exams?
No. There is no fixed answer key to memorize. Scenario-based exams score your reasoning in a live conversation, and system-based exams score what you actually do in the system. Dumps do not build the applied fluency the formats measure, and relying on them is a poor use of preparation time.
Are SAP certifications still worth it in 2026?
Yes, and arguably more so. The new formats make the credential harder to obtain and more meaningful to employers, because they prove applied skill rather than memorization. With the 2027 ECC end of mainstream maintenance deadline and rapid AI adoption, certified professionals who can deliver on real projects are in high demand.
How do I renew my SAP certification?
SAP certifications are valid for 12 months and are maintained through stay-certified assessments. SAP sends personalized notifications before your certification expires. Complete the assessment for your product area before the deadline to keep your credential current.
